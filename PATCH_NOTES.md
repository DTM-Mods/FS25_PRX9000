# Patch notes

## 1.7.0.0
- The silos now bring their own fill types (71 in total), so they add capacity for every crop and product without needing the Fed Mods pack. Types that already exist in the game or another mod are left untouched.

## 1.6.1.0
- Fixed: placed next to a Fed silo, the extension only added capacity for 10 grains.

## 1.6.0.2
- Shop pictures on a charcoal background.

## 1.6.0.1
- New shop icon with the updated silos.

## 1.6.0.0
- Fixes from the first in-game test on all three silos: new flat galvanised roof with moulded ribs (no more blue patches), solid elevator hopper, bolts and lifting rings no longer float, elevator set closer to the silo wall.

## 1.5.1.0
- New shop icon showing all three silos together.

## 1.5.0.0
- More detailed machinery: aeration blower, bucket elevator, conveyor and roof fill.

## 1.4.0.0
- Machinery kits added to all three silos.

## 1.3.0.0
- New corrugated wall look, with stiffening ribs up to half height.

## 1.2.1.0
- Walls, roof and concrete pad use the game's building shader, with new textures.

## 1.2.0.0
- Roof vent next to the top of the ladder.

## 1.1.0.0
- Two more sizes: PRX8000 (8 million L, $360,000) and PRX6000 (6 million L, $270,000), beside the PRX9000 (10 million L, $450,000).

## 1.0.0.0
- First release: PRX9000 silo extension, 18 m wide, 32 m tall, 10 million litres.
