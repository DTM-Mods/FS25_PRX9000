# PRX fix list (from in-game photos, 2026-10-29)

Every fix applies to EVERY silo that has the problem (PRX9000, PRX8000, PRX6000) - user, 2026-10-29.

## Problems reported
1. Elevator boot hopper: see-through walls (single-sided, no thickness). Planned: give the walls real thickness.
2. Elevator boot hopper: bolts floating in the air. Planned: move them onto the sloped side panels.
3. Hopper/leg doesn't touch the silo (about 5.25 m gap). Planned: move the leg in so the hopper end meets the wall, and shorten the conveyor to match.
4. Floating hoop above the aeration blower: REMOVED in source (not rebuilt yet).
5. Wall support ribs (base to top) are plain blocks with no detail. Should look like the reference photos: a formed
   channel profile with raised outer flanges and a recessed centre groove, a row of bolt heads running down it at
   regular spacing, and edges that catch the light. Reference photos: images 4, 5 and 6 from 2026-10-29.
6. Roof corrugation is a mishmash: lines run at different angles panel to panel, don't line up across the ring
   seams, and blue stripes/patches show at the seams. Sort it so the ribs run cleanly from the eave to the crown.
   USER (2026-10-29): roof is NOT corrugated. Flat galvanized steel panels, each with ONE rib down the middle,
   running from the middle of the silo to the outer edge.
7. Flat crown at the very top, where the fill collar bolts on: should NOT be corrugated. Make it one flat piece of
   steel that looks like real galvanized (spangle), not the corrugated roof texture.
8. Floating ring on top of the grain elevator head: REMOVED in source (not rebuilt yet).
9. Roof ring bands (the seams that circle the cone): blue patches/gaps break them up. Fill them in so each band is
   one continuous ring all the way around. Double-check all rings from several angles in the preview.
10. Second aeration fan (far side, 125 deg) REMOVED from all three kits; the one beside the ladder (-55 deg) stays.
    Not rebuilt yet.
11. Roof vent beside the ladder top is see-through: parts of the box/hood are missing or only drawn from one side.
    Make it a solid, closed vent from every angle.
